QUAN LY QUAY THUOC - C# WINFORMS (.NET 10)
Ban bo sung: NHAN VIEN + DON VI TINH + HOA DON

1. Giai nen ZIP va mo QuanLyQuanThuoc.slnx bang Visual Studio co ho tro .NET 10 (Windows Forms).
2. Neu Visual Studio hoi Restore NuGet Packages, dong y Restore.
3. Chay F5, dang nhap bang thong tin da co trong project goc.
4. Menu trai gom: Trang chu, Quan ly thuoc, Danh muc thuoc, Don vi tinh, Hoa don, Nhan vien.

A. DON VI TINH
- Co san 5 don vi mau: Vien, Hop, Lo, Chai, Tuyp (hien thi co dau trong form).
- Nhap ma don vi, ten don vi, mo ta; bam Them de them.
- Bam 1 hang trong bang de Sua/Xoa. Ma don vi khong doi khi sua.
- Xoa bi chan neu co thuoc dang su dung don vi do.
- Doi ten don vi se dong bo voi thuoc va luu sau khi thanh cong.
- Tim kiem theo ma, ten, mo ta; Lam moi de nhap moi.

B. HOA DON
- Nhap ma hoa don, ngay lap, ten khach, SDT (co the de trong), nhan vien ban, ghi chu.
- Chon mot thuoc, nhap so luong, bam '+ Them thuoc'; co the them nhieu dong.
- De bo dong: chon dong o bang CHI TIET, bam 'Xoa dong'.
- Tong tien tu dong tinh theo so luong * don gia cua thuoc.
- Bam Them de luu hoa don. De Sua/Xoa, chon mot hang o bang HOA DON (ben duoi).
- Tim kiem theo ma hoa don, khach hang, SDT, nhan vien; Lam moi de tao hoa don khac.
- Ma hoa don khong duoc trung. Du lieu hoa don giu ban chup gia, ten thuoc, nhan vien tai thoi diem luu.
- CHU Y: Hoa don CHUA TU DONG TRU TON KHO, CHUA IN HOA DON, CHUA KET NOI SQL SERVER.

C. QUAN LY THUOC
- Danh sach Don vi tinh cua form Quan ly thuoc lay tu menu Don vi tinh (khong con dong cung 5 muc).
- Them/Sua/Xoa thuoc duoc luu JSON (truoc day chi nam trong RAM). Ma thuoc khong doi khi sua.

D. NHAN VIEN
- Giu lai module Nhan vien cua ban truoc. Chon nhan vien dang lam khi tao hoa don.

FILE DU LIEU KHI CHAY (CHUA PHAT SINH THI KHONG CO FILE):
%LOCALAPPDATA%\QuanLyQuanThuoc\nhanvien.json
%LOCALAPPDATA%\QuanLyQuanThuoc\donvitinh.json
%LOCALAPPDATA%\QuanLyQuanThuoc\thuoc.json
%LOCALAPPDATA%\QuanLyQuanThuoc\hoadon.json

DU LIEU GOC:
- Thuoc, don vi va nhan vien co du lieu mau khi chua co JSON; hoa don ban dau rong.
- Co the chinh bo cuc tren Form...Designer.cs/Visual Studio Designer.
- Phien ban nay dung JSON de co the chay truc tiep ma khong can cai SQL Server.

CACH TEST NHANH:
1. Vao Don vi tinh, them DVT006 - Goi, xem xuat hien trong danh sach.
2. Vao Quan ly thuoc, them T004 - Thuoc test - Goi - So luong 10 - Don gia 15000.
3. Vao Hoa don, nhap HD001 - khach 'Nguyen Van A', chon nhan vien, them T004 so luong 2.
4. Kiem tra tong tien hien 30.000 d, bam Them, tim HD001 o bang hoa don.
5. Dong/mở lai ung dung; du lieu thuoc, don vi va hoa don phai con.

LUU Y:
- Chua co kha nang build/chay Windows Forms tren moi truong dong goi hien tai; ban can build tren Visual Studio Windows. Neu co loi build hay Designer, gui anh Error List de xu ly.
